Who we are

A sacred space, held without judgment.

Wrapped Up started because a few guys got honest with each other about a fight most men wage in total silence. Sexual sin and temptation thrive in isolation, so we built something to take that isolation away.

This place only works one way: without judgment, without condemnation, and with great sympathy and empathy toward how the enemy has come after us as men. We hold this space as sacred and confidential, a place to be truly authentic and vulnerable, when you're ready. You don't have to perform readiness. Listen, let the Spirit work in you as you hear what others share, and let's bear one another's burdens, freeing ourselves from these chains together.

We're not therapists and we're not perfect. We're brothers who show up, tell the truth, and point each other back to Christ when it would be easier to hide. No one here has it figured out. That's kind of the point.

If you're a man who's tired of fighting alone, there's a seat for you at the table.

No judgment
Honesty is met with grace, not shock or disgust.
Confidential
Names and stories are protected with care.
Real accountability
Compassion does not avoid hard truth.

Before anyone shares, leaders explain the group covenant and the safety and legal limits of confidentiality.

When you fail

Failure is never final.

Your failure is not a surprise to God. Do not disappear. Tell the truth quickly, call a brother, remove the opening, learn what led there, and return to the room.

What matters is how you respond next.

The real question

Do you want to be healed?

Lust can pose as comfort, escape, reward, or relief. Giving it up may feel like loss at first. What you gain is a life no longer controlled by secrecy and bondage.

Are you willing to be uncomfortable?

How it works

Simple, honest, consistent.

No performance and no curriculum to master. Just a clear rhythm for bringing the fight into the light.

01 · Show up

Come as you are

You do not need a clean week or a polished story. You only need to take the next honest step.

02 · Speak honestly

Say the true thing

We name the wins, the slips, the thoughts, and the temptations without comparison or shame.

03 · Walk together

Carry it as brothers

We check in, keep our word, and reach out before secrecy has time to take over.

Our format

Seven movements. One honest room.

Every gathering follows the same rhythm. It gives us enough structure to be honest and enough room for the Spirit to lead.

You can come and listen before you share.

  1. 01

    Worship

    Turn toward the Lord

    We begin with a song and prayer, bowing to the One who heals and asking the Spirit to move.

  2. 02

    Radical confession

    Bring it into the light

    We speak hidden thoughts and sins without graphic detail so secrecy can no longer rule from the dark.

  3. 03

    Accountability

    Name the pattern

    We review the four circles, name what moved us away from freedom, and strengthen weak guardrails.

  4. 04

    Practice

    Learn a better response

    We use a feelings wheel and gratitude to understand what sits beneath the urge and respond with wisdom.

  5. 05

    Prayer

    Stand with a brother

    We pray for chains to break, lies to lose their hold, and freedom to be experienced and stewarded.

  6. 06

    Expect

    Speak God’s truth

    We speak God’s promises of mercy, healing, restoration, refinement, and renewal over one another.

  7. 07

    Do

    Keep your word

    We leave with one clear commitment, practice daily gratitude, fast wisely, and stand on Ephesians 6.

What holds us together

Grace first. Then a different life.

Grace comes before behavior. Shame does not produce freedom.

Secrecy loses power in the light. Honest confession changes the fight.

Brotherhood is the discipline. Willpower was never meant to carry this alone.
